Abstract

Traditional chess engines rely upon deep alpha-beta search trees, Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS), and handcrafted or neural evaluation functions evaluated across millions of board positions per second. In this work, we present Nebium, an autoregressive chess language model that treats games as pure sequences of Universal Chess Interface (UCI) moves.

Nebium is built upon a modern decoder-only causal Transformer architecture incorporating Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE), SwiGLU non-linear activations, and Root Mean Square Normalization (RMSNorm). Pretrained on over 245,000+ competitive human games from Lichess without search or heuristics, Nebium learns both the hard constraints of chess rules (>98.9% move legality) and deep strategic motifs.

Crucially, to evaluate the model beyond surface next-token loss, we implement downstream behavioral evaluations using the Lichess puzzle database stratified by rating brackets (<1500, 1500-2000, 2000+). This allows measuring whether the model predicts the objectively best move in critical tactical positions rather than merely matching human frequency. Finally, we provide native GGUF export for local, latency-critical inference on standard CPU and edge hardware.

Model Architecture & Training Pipeline

The training pipeline takes raw PGN game records, extracts sanitized UCI move streams, and trains a specialized Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) tokenizer that preserves full UCI tokens (such as e2e4, g1f3). The autoregressive Transformer processes these tokens through stacked causal multi-head attention layers conditioned with Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE) to maintain relative positional distance between early opening maneuvers and endgame maneuvers.

Behavioral Tactics Evaluation

Next-token accuracy conflates "predicting what an average player did" with "predicting the objectively best move." To overcome this limitation, Nebium evaluates against the Lichess Tactics Database.

Qualitative Progression Across Training

Early epochs exhibit syntactic exploration of square coordinates with frequent illegal moves. By mid-training (epochs 5–10), the model firmly establishes piece geometry, castling conditions, and standard theoretical opening paths. By epoch 20+, Nebium executes sharp tactical combinations and defensive resources.
